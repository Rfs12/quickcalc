Frequently Asked Questions

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan lets you invest a fixed amount in mutual funds every month, benefiting from rupee-cost averaging and compounding.

What return should I expect?

Equity mutual funds have historically returned around 10–12% annually over long periods, but returns are not guaranteed.
