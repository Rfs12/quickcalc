Frequently Asked Questions

What are CGST and SGST?

For intra-state sales in India, GST is split equally into CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST) — each is half the total GST rate.

How do I remove GST from a total?

Choose 'Remove GST (inclusive)', enter the total price and rate — the calculator extracts the base price and GST amount.
