About this word counter

Free online word counter — count words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences and estimated reading time as you type.. This tool is completely free, works on any device — mobile, tablet or desktop — and requires no signup. All calculations happen instantly in your browser, so your data never leaves your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a word limit?

No — paste text of any length; counts update instantly as you type.

How is reading time calculated?

Based on an average adult reading speed of about 200 words per minute.
